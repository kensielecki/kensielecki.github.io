Automation

SF/Bay Area Crypto Events Aggregator

Finding the right events in SF is a full-time job across six fragmented platforms. This tool scrapes Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup, AgendaHero, Cerebral Valley, and FinTech Week SF simultaneously, deduplicates the output, and feeds every event to Claude alongside a detailed profile of deal-relevant preferences — crypto, DeFi, AI agents, institutional finance, senior-level audiences. Claude scores each event 1–10 against that profile with reasoning, then outputs a ranked weekly digest delivered directly to Telegram. No manual trawling, no noise — just the ten events worth attending.

Roadmap: add Notion pages and Google Sheets as additional sources, so conference schedules and curated lists from contacts can feed the same pipeline.

04 — 2026
BD Automation

Correspondence Intelligence — Telegram & Gmail

Most crypto BD lives in two places: Telegram DMs and Gmail threads. These tools cover both ends. The Telegram module handles outbound — bulk messaging to segmented contact lists using your actual Telegram account via the MTProto API, with a password-protected local web UI for managing lists, scheduling sends, and tracking delivery without touching code. Built for conference follow-up, partner outreach, and deal flow broadcasting; messages land in DMs from your own account, not a bot.

The Gmail module handles inbound intelligence: it reads your email threads, extracts relationship context from past conversations, and surfaces Claude-drafted replies that match your tone and the thread history — so you can review, edit, and send without context-switching. Together they form a lightweight correspondence stack: outbound broadcast via Telegram, inbound triage and drafting across both channels.

05 — 2026
DeFi Analytics

DeFi Yield Dashboard & Alerts

Monitors lending rates and yield opportunities across DeFi via DefiLlama. Diffs daily snapshots and fires Telegram alerts when yields shift beyond a configurable threshold — so significant moves in Aave, Morpho, or Pendle don't go unnoticed.

The core metric tracked is the carry spread between on-chain yield and the US 10-year Treasury. When that spread compresses below a threshold, the risk-adjusted case for DeFi yield weakens. Built on the same data pipeline as the valuation screener.
